NXYMusic is an independent, community-maintained music app for Android.

<br><b>Features:</b>

- Discover and play music.
- Organize your library and playlists.
- View synchronized lyrics from available providers.
- Use supported offline playback and playback controls.
- Access integrations supported by your device and app configuration.

Features and availability may depend on your device, app settings, and third-party services. NXYMusic is independent and is not affiliated with Google, YouTube, or YouTube Music.

NXYMusic is open-source software distributed under GPL-3.0. Source code and signed releases: https://github.com/revhuyrr/nxymuisc
